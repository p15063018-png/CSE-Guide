About CSE Video Guide

CSE Video Guide helps students quickly discover high-quality learning videos for core CSE subjects. Filter by language and difficulty, track your progress, and build a personalized learning path.

← Back to Home

Why this app exists

Many CSE students struggle to find the right videos among thousands of options. This app solves that problem by organizing videos by subject, topic, language, and difficulty, and by letting students track what they have saved and completed.

Key features

  • Subject-based video discovery (Python, DBMS, Data Structures)
  • Language and difficulty filters
  • Search by topic or title
  • Sort by duration or difficulty
  • Save favorite videos
  • Mark videos as completed
  • Learning progress bar
  • Dark mode for comfortable viewing
  • Video details popup

Technologies used

This project is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Data is stored in the browser using localStorage.
